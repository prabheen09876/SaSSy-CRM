# Prepare your deployment's privacy notice

This repository does not ship a finished privacy policy. The page at `public/privacy/index.html` is an explicitly unconfigured setup notice, not a statement about a particular business.

Before accepting real customer records, the platform operator must publish an accurate notice for that deployment. Review at least:

- The responsible business/legal entity and working privacy contact.
- Personal information collected, purposes, and applicable legal bases.
- Hosting, authentication, analytics, and messaging providers actually used.
- Business/workspace access and disclosures to authorized staff or providers.
- Retention, deletion, access/export requests, and backup handling.
- Cookies/browser storage and security practices actually implemented.
- International transfers and jurisdiction-specific rights where applicable.
- Messaging consent, unsubscribe/opt-out handling, and WhatsApp/Brevo processing if installed.
- Effective date and how changes are communicated.

Use approved business details, not another installation's contacts. Have the completed notice reviewed for the product's actual data flows and applicable requirements. Neither this checklist nor row-level security establishes legal compliance.
